Welcome to the Vybe Study

Help us understand first-time experiences with a relationship app

Thank you for participating in this study! You'll be downloading and exploring a real app called Vybe while screen recording your phone.

This is not a test. There are no right or wrong answers. We are studying the app, not you. If something is confusing or unclear, that's exactly the kind of feedback we need. Your honest reactions — positive or negative — are what make this study valuable.

What you'll do

  1. Answer a few background questions
  2. Download the free Vybe app on your phone
  3. Screen record as you complete guided tasks
  4. Send a real invitation to someone you know
  5. Answer a questionnaire about your experience
  6. Upload your screen recording

2 Setup

Before exploring Vybe, let's get your phone ready.

Download Vybe

Search for "Vybe" in the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android) and install it. Do NOT open it yet.

Start screen recording

iPhone: Open Control Center (swipe down from top-right corner). Tap the screen recording button (circle icon). You'll see a 3-second countdown, then recording starts.

If you don't see it, go to Settings > Control Center and add "Screen Recording."

To enable audio narration: long-press the screen recording button and turn on Microphone before starting.

Android: Swipe down from the top of your screen to open Quick Settings. Tap "Screen Recorder" or "Screen Record."

To enable audio narration: When prompted, choose to record audio from your microphone.

We'd love to hear you think aloud. Narrating your thoughts as you use the app is the most valuable part of the recording — say what you notice, what you expect, and what confuses you. Enable your microphone before you start recording.

If you'd rather not narrate, that's OK. You'll have the option to type your thoughts at key moments instead.
